Frequently Asked Questions

How do I scan a receipt?

Tap the gold + button on the Dashboard or Invoices tab. Point your camera at any receipt and HisabPro will automatically extract the vendor, amount, and VAT. Review, correct if needed, and tap Save.

How does the VAT 201 report work?

Go to Reports → select a quarter → tap Generate FTA Report. HisabPro calculates your input/output VAT and produces a PDF matching UAE FTA form 201, ready to file.

Is my data backed up?

Yes — if you're signed into iCloud on your device, HisabPro syncs automatically to your private iCloud account using Apple CloudKit. It's end-to-end encrypted and only you can access it.

What is the free plan limit?

The free plan allows 10 invoices per month. Upgrade to Pro or Business for unlimited invoices, PDF export, and CSV export.

How do I restore my subscription after reinstalling?

Go to Settings → Subscription → tap "Restore Purchase". Your subscription is tied to your Apple ID and restores automatically.

How do I delete all my data?

Go to Settings → Export All Data first (to keep a backup), then delete HisabPro from your iPhone. All local data is removed with the app.

Privacy

Read our Privacy Policy. Short version: HisabPro has no servers, no analytics, and cannot see your data.
