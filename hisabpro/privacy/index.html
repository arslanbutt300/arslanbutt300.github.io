Privacy Policy

HisabPro — UAE Bookkeeping & VAT

Last updated: June 8, 2026

Plain English: HisabPro stores everything on your device. We have no server, no analytics, no tracking. The only data leaving your device is iCloud sync (via Apple's encrypted infrastructure) and purchase receipts handled by Apple StoreKit.

Data We Collect

None. HisabPro has no backend, no analytics SDK, and no crash-reporting service that sends data to us. We literally cannot see your data.

Data You Store Locally

  • Business name, TRN number, phone, business type
  • Invoice records (vendor, amount, VAT, date, category, notes)
  • Invoice photos (stored in app's private Documents directory)
  • Quarterly VAT aggregates and summaries
  • App preferences (language, theme, biometric lock)

iCloud Sync

If you are signed into iCloud on your device, HisabPro syncs your data to your personal iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit private database. This data is end-to-end encrypted and only accessible to you. Apple is the data processor — we never see, access, or control this data.

Subscriptions

In-app purchase receipts are handled entirely by Apple StoreKit. We do not store your payment information. We receive only a confirmation of active subscription tier from StoreKit.

App Permissions

PermissionWhy We Need It
CameraTo scan receipts and extract invoice details via OCR. Never accessed in background.

Face ID / Touch IDOptional. To lock the app and protect your business records.

Third-Party Services

None embedded in the app. The only third parties are Apple itself (StoreKit for subscriptions, iCloud/CloudKit for optional backup).

Children's Privacy

HisabPro is a business tool not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because we do not collect data from anyone.

Your Rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, delete, or rectify. To delete all your data:

  • Open HisabPro → Settings → Export All Data (optional backup first)
  • Delete HisabPro from your iPhone

All on-device data is deleted with the app. iCloud-synced data follows Apple's iCloud deletion policy.

Changes

If we ever begin collecting data (we have no plans to), we will require explicit in-app opt-in and update this policy at least 30 days before any change takes effect.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us via WhatsApp support as shown in the app Settings, or email arslanbutt300@gmail.com.
